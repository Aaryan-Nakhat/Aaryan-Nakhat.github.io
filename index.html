Equity Research · Forensic Analysis · LLM

Personal Equity-Research & Forensic Analysis Workbench (Indian Markets)

An end-to-end equity-research & forensic-analysis platform for Indian (NSE/BSE) stocks, built strictly on primary/official sources. Anti-bot scrapers pull exchange filings, XBRL financials, live quotes, F&O participant OI, FII/DII flows, USD/INR, MCX commodities, sectoral-index OHLC/valuation, and ~14,500 AMFI mutual-fund NAVs into a 15+-table DuckDB store. A deterministic engine layers multi-year statements, cash-flow quality, and a forensic block (Altman Z, Beneish M, Sloan accruals, Piotroski) with sector-aware valuation (P/B-on-ROE, EV/EBITDA, Monte-Carlo DCF, reverse-DCF). Adds holder-level ownership tracking with quarter-over-quarter change and smart-money cost-zone / profit-booking-risk inference, plus a stack of idea-discovery & signal engines — a Nifty-500 value finder, a holding-company discount finder, a ~25 marquee-investor tracker, a capex-led small-cap hunter, a government policy radar over primary PIB releases, a top-down sector-rotation analyzer, technical level/breakout screens, a portfolio sell-advisor, and "Tailwind" — an autonomous multi-agent engine that reads global policy / export-ban / tariff shocks (Google News + US Federal Register) and traces them to verified Indian beneficiary producers — plus fund deep-reports (~14,500 schemes) and IPO pre-listing notes. An LLM turns filings/concalls into a forensic investment thesis, served by an always-on email/Telegram bot — on-demand PDF reports plus live pre-market + midday + EOD digests with movers, FII positioning, and insider/promoter (SEBI PIT) signals, and weekly Tailwind & sector pushes.
